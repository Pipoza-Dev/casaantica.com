Our Founding Manifesto

The Authentic Woodcraft Guild

Founded in 2011, CASA ANTICA unites generational woodworking artisans with thoughtful architectural design. We build heirloom furniture designed to live with your family for generations.

The Rejection of Fast Disposable Furniture

Modern furniture retail became overwhelmed with hollow particle board flat-packs designed to fail after a couple of moves.

CASA ANTICA chose authentic permanence. We source whole logs of sustainable American Walnut, Burmese Teak, and White Oak, season them in our specialized solar-assisted kilns to exactly 9% moisture content, and handcraft each piece with butterfly keys and Japanese blind joinery.
