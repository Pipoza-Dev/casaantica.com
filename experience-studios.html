Studio Visit Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Are walk-in visits welcome?

Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome during regular gallery hours. However, scheduling a private reservation guarantees an assigned senior design consultant and dedicated time at the timber swatches table.

Can I take timber and fabric samples home?

Absolutely. We provide all registered visitors with our complimentary Curated Timber & Swatch Box containing finished hardwood samples and fabric swatches so you can test them under your home’s exact lighting.

Is valet parking available?

Yes. All our studios (Surat VIP Road, Bangalore Indiranagar, Mumbai Bandra West, and Noida Sector 62) offer complimentary valet parking or dedicated reserved customer parking bays.

Can I customize dimensions seen on display?

Every single piece in our collections is made-to-order. You can tailor length, width, timber species, edge profile, and upholstery fabrics to millimeter precision for your floor plan.
