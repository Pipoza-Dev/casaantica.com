Master Joinery & Construction Details

Kiln-Dried Moisture Balancing

Every plank undergoes a 4-week computer-controlled kiln cycle bringing moisture content to exactly 9%. This prevents bowing, hairline cracks, or warping even in coastal humidity.

Traditional Japanese Mortise & Tenon

Zero corner brackets or fragile nails. Interlocking wood joints flex naturally with temperature cycles, guaranteeing a foundation that comfortably survives generations.

Zero-VOC Organic Oil Patina

Hand-burnished with cold-pressed linseed oils, carnauba wax, and bee propolis. Protects against water rings without building an artificial plastic film over the wood.
